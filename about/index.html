About CatchUp Recruiting

Led by Ayan Nasir, in Houston, Texas.

CatchUp Recruiting is a construction recruiting firm focused on helping companies hire the right project and operational talent for active, demanding jobs. The business was built with a practical understanding of construction from the inside, not just from a staffing perspective. Led by Ayan Nasir, whose background includes project management experience in commercial and mission critical construction, CatchUp Recruiting focuses on identifying candidates who are not only qualified on paper, but who also understand what is required to succeed on real projects.

Ayan Nasir, Founder

Houston, Texas. Ayan's background is project management in commercial and mission critical construction. That field experience is why CatchUp exists. Ayan handles client conversations, commercial terms and the relationship with each hiring company.

Years of experience, employers and project types stay off this page until Ayan confirms the exact facts in writing.

What we do and do not do

We recruit contractor side project and operational roles in Texas, in four sectors, as direct hire paid by the hiring company.

We do not do general staffing, temp work, light industrial or admin.
