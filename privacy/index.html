Privacy

What happens to your details.

Draft. This page needs review by a professional before launch.

What we collect

The details you type into the forms (name, email, phone, location, work history answers, resume), and basic visit information such as which page you came from.

Why

To consider you for contractor side roles and to contact you. For hiring companies, to reply to your spec.

Who sees it

The CatchUp team. A hiring company sees your details only with your agreement.

How long we keep it

Retention period to be decided by Ayan with advice. You can ask us to delete your details at any time by email. Address to be added once tested.

What we do not ask for

We do not ask for your Social Security number, date of birth, race, sex or national origin, or visa category or citizenship status.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to see how the site is used.
