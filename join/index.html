For candidates

Tell us what you actually ran.

CatchUp recruits contractor side project managers, estimators and superintendents in Texas. A title tells us very little. What the company self performed, what you personally owned and where you handed off tells us a lot. Joining the talent network puts that on file.

What to know before you join

  • We do not list open roles on this site. When a role matches what you have run, we contact you directly.
  • We tell you plainly when something is not a fit.
  • When there is no update, we tell you there is no update, on the day it becomes clear.
  • It costs you nothing.

What happens after you join

  1. You receive a short set of written questions about your projects. They are the same for everyone.

  2. If your scope fits, a thirty minute call.

  3. You are put forward to a contractor only with your agreement.

Who the network is for

Project managers, senior project managers, assistant project managers, estimators, senior estimators and superintendents who work for a general contractor, a self perform contractor or a specialty subcontractor. Based in Texas, or with a specific plan to relocate.

Design side, engineer of record, owner's representative and corporate real estate backgrounds are different disciplines from the roles contractors hire for. We say so rather than leave you waiting.
