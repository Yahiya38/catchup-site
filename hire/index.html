For contractors

Your job description may be screening for the wrong thing.

A generic spec asks for a degree, PMP preferred, five plus years, strong communication and software proficiency. Every line is defensible. Together they screen for almost nothing, because years measure time, not ownership.

What happens when you send it

  1. You send the spec.

    Paste it or upload it. Tell us whether the seat is open now or opens with an award.

  2. We mark it up.

    We separate the true must haves from the stated requirements and tell you what the spec is screening for against what it should be. We aim to send the mark up back within a day.

  3. Ayan joins.

    Ayan Nasir, who runs CatchUp and handles the commercial side, joins so you are talking to the right person on anything beyond the spec itself.

There is no charge for the mark up.

Example only

What a mark up looks like

The spec says

  • Five plus years
  • Degree
  • Strong communication
  • Data center experience

We ask instead

  • What did the company self perform on those projects?
  • Who wrote and negotiated the change orders?
  • Was cost forecasting independent, or supported?
  • How far into commissioning did they stay?

How we search

Every search starts from your spec. We search on scope: what the company self performed, what the person owned, where they handed off. We search with exclusions as carefully as inclusions, so design side, engineer of record, owner's representative and consultant profiles do not reach your desk.

What we tell you up front

If the spec asks for something the market does not hold, we say so before we search. If it screens for the wrong thing, we say that too. If a person is not a fit, we tell them plainly rather than leave them waiting.

Who we work with

General contractors, self perform contractors and specialty subcontractors in Texas, hiring in data center and mission critical, heavy civil, transportation or industrial work.

Questions hiring companies ask

What does a construction recruiter do?

A construction recruiter finds and screens people for roles on a contractor's projects. CatchUp screens on scope, meaning what the company self performed, what the person owned and where they handed off, before looking at title or years.

Which roles does CatchUp recruit?

Project Manager, Senior Project Manager, Assistant Project Manager, Estimator, Senior Estimator and Superintendent, on the contractor side.

How does CatchUp screen?

On scope before titles. See how we screen.
