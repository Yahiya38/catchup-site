Scope over titles

A generic spec screens for almost nothing. Three questions do the filtering.

  1. What did the company self perform?Running your own crews and managing subcontractors are different jobs with the same title.
  2. What did this person personally own?Project value hides whether they ran the job or held one package alongside five other project managers.
  3. Where did they hand off?At substantial completion, or through integrated systems testing.

Hiring follows power.

Capital moves, grid capacity does not. A project with an interconnection moves, and a project without one waits, whatever capital is behind it. So hiring concentrates in specific corridors, and two contractors an hour apart can be in completely different hiring conditions.

Questions people ask

What does a construction recruiter do?

A construction recruiter finds and screens people for roles on a contractor's projects. CatchUp screens on scope, meaning what the company self performed, what the person owned and where they handed off, before looking at title or years.

Which part of the country do you cover?

Texas only. Dallas Fort Worth, Austin, San Antonio, Houston and West Texas.

Do you recruit for design firms or owner's representatives?

No. CatchUp recruits contractor side roles only.
