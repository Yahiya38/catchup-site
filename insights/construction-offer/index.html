What is really inside a construction offer

CatchUp Recruiting. 1 November 2026.

Base salary is the least informative number in a construction offer. Two offers with the same base can be very different jobs to accept.

What the package includes

A truck allowance or company vehicle. Per diem on travel heavy roles. A bonus, frequently tied to project margin or completion rather than company performance. Profit sharing with vesting conditions. Relocation that may be clawed back inside 12 or 24 months.

The useful question

Ask what the bonus actually paid out at last year, not what it is targeted at.

Why counteroffers are rarely about money

This is something we have observed, not data. The common real reasons are an unrecovered schedule nobody acknowledged, a senior seat that went elsewhere, or travel that stopped being sustainable.
