Why a job spec screens for the wrong thing

CatchUp Recruiting. 1 November 2026.

A generic spec asks for a degree, PMP preferred, five plus years, strong communication and software proficiency. Every line is defensible. Together they screen for almost nothing, because years measure time, not ownership.

Three questions do the filtering

What did the company self perform, versus manage subcontractors? Running your own crews and managing subcontractors are different jobs with the same title.

What did the person personally own, versus route or review? Project value hides whether they ran the job or held one package alongside five other project managers.

Where did they hand off, at substantial completion, or through integrated systems testing?

Hire against backlog

This is something we have observed, not data. Hiring against backlog beats hiring against vacancy. The seat opens when the award is likely, not when someone resigns.
