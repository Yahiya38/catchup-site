How far into commissioning did they stay

CatchUp Recruiting. 1 November 2026.

Commercial and mission critical projects of identical value look identical on a resume. They diverge at the point where systems have to prove they work.

Where the jobs diverge

On a commercial job, substantial completion is largely a punch list conversation. On a mission critical job it means Level 4 and Level 5 commissioning, integrated systems testing and load bank testing against redundancy requirements that do not move. The owner is asking whether the building fails safely.

The question to ask

Ask how far into commissioning someone stayed. Many strong project managers have built on data center sites without touching critical systems: shell and core, sitework, admin fit out. Good work, different job.
