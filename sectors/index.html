Sectors and Texas

Four sectors, and only these four.

CatchUp is not a general staffing firm. We recruit project and operational talent in four sectors for active, demanding jobs, and only in Texas.

Data Center and Mission Critical

Commercial and mission critical projects of identical value look identical on a resume. They diverge where systems have to prove they work: Level 4 and Level 5 commissioning, integrated systems testing, load bank testing. We ask how far into commissioning someone stayed.

Heavy Civil

Mass excavation, grading and utilities. A project manager who ran self perform crews and one who managed subcontractors can hold the same title and have done different jobs.

Transportation

Public infrastructure work, where bid and award cycles decide when a seat opens. Hiring against backlog beats hiring against vacancy: the seat opens when the award is likely, not when someone resigns.

Industrial

Industrial work where uptime pressure and critical systems already exist. That is why high tech manufacturing and heavy industrial backgrounds are often the fastest route into mission critical work. This is something we have observed, not a statistic.

Texas, corridor by corridor

Hiring follows power. Capital moves, grid capacity does not. A project with an interconnection moves, and a project without one waits, whatever capital sits behind it. Hiring concentrates in specific corridors, and two contractors an hour apart can be in completely different hiring conditions.

So the number that matters is not how many project managers there are in Texas. It is how many are uncommitted inside the corridor where a project has power.

Where we work: Dallas Fort Worth, Austin, San Antonio, Houston, and West Texas, which matters for data center siting.
